White Screen Test

Click Go Fullscreen to check your monitor, laptop or phone for dead pixels, stuck pixels and uneven backlight. Free, no ads, no install.

How to run a white screen test

  1. Clean your screen first, so dust is not mistaken for a defect.
  2. Set brightness to maximum and click Go Fullscreen.
  3. Look for dark or coloured dots, dim patches and glowing edges.
  4. Click or press the right arrow to cycle black, red, green and blue. Each colour reveals different faults.
  5. Press Esc to exit.

What a white screen shows

Dead pixels

A pixel that never lights up. It appears as a small black dot on a white screen.

Stuck pixels

A pixel stuck on one colour (red, green, blue or white). It is easiest to spot on a black screen.

Backlight bleed and uneven brightness

Light leaking from the edges, or patches that are brighter or dimmer than the rest. A white screen in a dark room shows this best.

Other uses

Use it as a light source for video calls or photos, a clean background for recording, or to spot smudges when cleaning. See the screen cleaning page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I test my screen for dead pixels?

Open this page, click Go Fullscreen, and look carefully at a white screen, then black, red, green and blue. Any dot that stays the same colour is a faulty pixel.

What is the difference between a dead pixel and a stuck pixel?

A dead pixel is always off and looks black. A stuck pixel is always on and shows one colour.

Can dead pixels be fixed?

Stuck pixels sometimes recover with gentle pressure or pixel-refresh videos. Dead pixels usually cannot be fixed. If your device is under warranty, check the manufacturer's pixel policy.

Does this work on phones?

Yes. Open the page, tap Go Fullscreen and swipe or tap to change colours.
