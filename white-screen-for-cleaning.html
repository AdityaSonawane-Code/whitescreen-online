White Screen for Cleaning

A bright white screen makes dust and fingerprints easy to see. Go fullscreen, then clean.

How to clean your screen safely

  1. Switch the device off or show a white screen and turn brightness up.
  2. Unplug a monitor or laptop if possible.
  3. Wipe gently with a dry microfibre cloth.
  4. For stubborn marks, slightly dampen the cloth with water. Never spray liquid directly on the screen.
  5. Avoid paper towels, alcohol-heavy wipes and ammonia cleaners, which can damage coatings.

Check again on a white screen, then on black for any remaining marks.

Frequently asked questions

Why use a white screen for cleaning?

Dust and smudges show as dark or glossy spots on a bright background.

What should I use to clean my monitor?

A clean dry microfibre cloth, lightly dampened with water if needed.

Can I use glass cleaner?

No. Many glass cleaners damage anti-glare and anti-reflective coatings.
