Full Screen White Screen

One click for a full white screen. Adjust brightness or switch to a warm colour for lighting.

Ways to use a full white screen

  • Video call light: a white or warm screen softens shadows on your face. Use the warm light colour to reduce eye strain.
  • Photography: a clean white backdrop for small product shots.
  • Reading light: a dim warm screen at night.
  • Screen recording: a plain background for tutorials.

Get the image

Choose a size under the preview and click Download to save a plain colour PNG at that resolution.
