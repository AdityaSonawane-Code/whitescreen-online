Black Screen Test

Display a pure black screen to find stuck pixels and backlight bleed. Best used in a dark room.

What a black screen reveals

  • Stuck pixels: bright dots that never turn off.
  • Backlight bleed: light leaking along the edges of LCD panels.
  • IPS glow: a soft glow in the corners that shifts when you move your head.
  • Black level: OLED screens should look completely black.

Tips

Turn off room lights, set brightness to about 70 to 100 percent and view from a normal distance. Slight corner glow on IPS panels is normal. Strong patches or bright streaks are worth a warranty check.

Frequently asked questions

What is backlight bleed?

Light from the LED backlight leaking around the panel edge. It is most visible on black screens.

Is IPS glow a defect?

No. It is a normal characteristic of IPS panels, though its strength varies between units.

Why is my black screen not fully black?

LCD screens always emit some backlight. Only OLED screens can turn pixels fully off.
