Dead Pixel Test

Cycle through full-screen colours to find dead and stuck pixels. Click Go Fullscreen, then click or press → to change colour.

How the dead pixel test works

Every pixel is made of red, green and blue sub-pixels. Showing one solid colour at a time makes a faulty sub-pixel stand out.

  • White: dead pixels appear as black dots.
  • Black: stuck bright pixels appear as white or coloured dots.
  • Red, green, blue: reveal faulty sub-pixels and colour patches.

Steps

  1. Clean the screen and set brightness to maximum.
  2. Go fullscreen and move your face close to the screen.
  3. Scan each colour slowly, including corners and edges.
  4. Note the position of any faulty pixel. Contact the seller if it is under warranty.

Frequently asked questions

How many dead pixels are acceptable?

It depends on the manufacturer. Many follow ISO 13406-2 classes, and some allow a few faulty pixels before a warranty claim.

Why is there a dot that only shows on one colour?

That is usually a faulty sub-pixel, for example a red sub-pixel stuck off.

Is a dead pixel test safe for my screen?

Yes. It only displays solid colours, like any normal image.
